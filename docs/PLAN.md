# Kingdom Chaos: Crown Quest — Implementation Notes

The Crown Quest rules replace the earlier Dragonfall rules. Where they conflicted, Crown Quest wins.
In particular, the earlier Glory-based win and the mine-with-an-action gems are gone.

## What changed from Dragonfall

| Area | Dragonfall | Crown Quest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board | Free-form path graph, any direction, stop early | 40-space two-way main road with a house plate at each gate, four castles with gates and forks, four 5-space Royal Paths to the central temple, inner routes (ring, tunnels, ruins, workshop, piers, lake) with perks and risks, separate dungeon map |
| Movement | Up to N steps, tap stone by stone | Whole roll on the island (dungeon may stop early); choose an end space, preview, confirm |
| Gems | Mine with an adventure action, bank inside the castle | Picked up when passing or landing on a station, banked when passing or landing on your gate |
| Win | Four gems and a cut, then reach the temple | Four gems and a cut, plus one piece touching all four house plates, then its own Royal Path with an exact landing |
| Battles | Challenge, Battle Cry, reaction window | Removed. Cuts by movement only; Shield and Immunity make a landing illegal |
| Cards | 17 types, hand 3 | 18 types, 35 cards, hand 4, discard-or-decline on a full hand |
| Tutorial | 10 steps | 11 steps on the new board |

The GameRunner, Socket.IO rooms, reconnection, host transfer, rate limits, settings, sound and
menus were kept. The protocol changed only in its action schema.

## Architecture

```
src/engine/
  crownLayout.ts   Hand-placed anchors in image pixels: circuit anchors, houses, features, side routes, dungeon rooms
  crownRoutes.ts   Circuit polylines traced from the painting (generated by scripts/trace-island.ts)
  crownquest.ts    Builds nodes and edges: circuit, castles, gates, final paths, temple, side routes, dungeon
  board.ts         Board registry and lookups (adjacency, outgoing edges, houses by seat)
  graph.ts         Edge open/blocked state, connectivity checks, distances
  config.ts        GameConfig (every balance number, deck, discoveries), ROOM_CONFIG, match pace presets
  types.ts         GameState, actions, events, views
  rules.ts         Movement enumeration, walking, laps, gems, cuts, teleports, retreat, results
  cards.ts         Card registry: category, targets, effects
  queries.ts       legalMoves, movableCharacters, noMoveReason, adventureOptions, playableCards, objectiveText
  engine.ts        createGame / applyAction / forceTimeout / tick
  view.ts          Per-player redaction
  ai.ts            Practice AI (own view only)
  rulesText.ts     Single source for all rule, card, character, location and status text
src/lib/movePlan.ts    Destination preview: ends, routes, consequences, whyNot
```

The UI never decides rules. Every option it shows comes from engine queries, and the server
re-validates every action.

## Key decisions on ambiguous points

- **Leaving home.** Any roll leaves the castle, and the first step is the gate. The castle counts as
  the space before the gate when the preview explains distances.
- **Pickup.** A piece only picks up a gem its house still needs, and not one another of its own
  pieces already carries. Duplicates go back to the station.
- **Carry limit.** A piece carries up to `carryLimit` gems (default 2, chosen to shorten matches; the
  spec's original rule is 1). Every carried gem banks at the gate.
- **Steal.** A cut takes the victim's gems while the attacker has free hands. The rest drop on the space.
- **Laps are house plates.** A piece touches the four gate plates in any order by passing or
  landing. Teleports, swaps, vehicles and shortcuts count only a destination plate. Traps keep
  plates; a cut or return home resets them.
- **Movement.** The main road is two-way; the whole roll (after the dragon penalty) is compulsory
  and a move may not revisit a space. With no legal full move, the move is skipped.
- **Extra turns.** A natural 6 or a movement cut earns one extra turn (both together: one), taken
  after the current turn; chains are allowed.
- **Knight.** He cuts only the first unprotected opponent he passes. A protected one blocks him like
  anyone else.
- **Shield and Immunity** last until the start of their owner's next turn. Both make landing illegal.
  Immunity also blocks Swap.
- **Dragon recovery** lasts until each other player has had a turn.
- **Map cards** only act on marked sites and are refused if any piece would be cut off from the
  board.
- **Full hand.** The player picks a discard before the action (`discardCardId`). With none picked,
  the new card is declined. A modal at the moment of the draw would need a pending-choice state,
  which the engine avoids so turns stay atomic.
- **Timeouts.** The die is rolled if needed, the AI picks a move, the adventure action is skipped and
  the turn passes.
- **Ties.** Most banked gems, then having made a cut, then shared.
- **Tutorial opponent.** In the tutorial, Alex's turns are played by the local AI so multi-turn lessons
  (the dungeon) work. It never touches online rooms.
- **In-memory rooms.** A single process holds the rooms, and they are lost on restart.

## Open design question

Simulated matches are far longer than the 15-minute target (see the README). Carrying two gems is
applied by default and saves roughly 15–25% of the turns. Untried levers:

- bank at any gate;
- a shorter circuit;
- fewer required gems as a configurable preset.

Seat balance is also off (Purple wins about half of 4-player simulations, Blue under 10%).
